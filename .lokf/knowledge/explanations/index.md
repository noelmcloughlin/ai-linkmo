# Explanations

Understanding-oriented concepts - the reasoning behind the repository, as distinct from descriptions of its machinery:

* [Why linked AI governance](why-linked-ai-governance.md) - Why AI-LinkMO keeps AI governance as one catalogue of linked data behind four doors (the CLI, the API, the web UI and the graph database): the problems of governance kept in PDFs and spreadsheets, and why one shared ontology makes the traceability auditable.
* [What AI-LinkMO adds to AI Atlas Nexus](what-ai-linkmo-adds.md) - AI-LinkMO is a workshop built on IBM AI Atlas Nexus. It adds doors, encodings and checks but no ontology of its own, and its governance records carry no provenance yet.
