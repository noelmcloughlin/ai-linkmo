# Glossary

The domain vocabulary of AI-LinkMO, as defined in the root `README.md` and carried by the [AI Risk Ontology](../references/ai-risk-ontology.md):

* [Taxonomy](taxonomy.md) - a framework's catalogue of risks/controls.
* [Risk](risk.md) - a named, identified harm.
* [Control / Action](control-action.md) - something you do or deploy to detect or mitigate a risk.
* [Obligation](obligation.md) - a requirement a framework imposes, with evidence categories.
* [Crosswalk](crosswalk.md) - a machine-generated mapping of equivalent concepts between frameworks.
* [Bring Your Own Data (BYOD)](byod.md) - internal policies encoded in the same schema.
