# Evaluation

An **evaluation** is a benchmark or test that measures a [risk](risk.md) or a capability, linked to the datasets and tasks it uses.

The `/evaluation` endpoint and `./ai evaluation` serve them from the packaged ai-atlas-nexus evaluation data, for example `ai_eval_PopQA`. An evaluation is how a risk named in a [taxonomy](taxonomy.md) becomes something measurable on a particular model.
